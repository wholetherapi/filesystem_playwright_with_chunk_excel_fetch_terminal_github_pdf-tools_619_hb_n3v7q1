# Fluid Systems — Inline Flow Meters — certification requirements

- **Track ID:** FS-11
- **Owner:** Fluid Systems Division (FSD)
- **Level:** division / track
- **Conformance profile:** RIC-FS-2.2
- **Last revised:** 2026-02-09

## Minimum field validation

Applicants for the Fluid Systems — Inline Flow Meters track must document at
least **68 supervised field-validation hours** on a flow
calibration loop.

## Evidence checklist

1. Field-validation log (RIC-LOG-09).
2. Flow-loop calibration certificate.
3. Quality-function countersignature.
