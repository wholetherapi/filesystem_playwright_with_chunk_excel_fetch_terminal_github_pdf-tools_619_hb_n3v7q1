# Rotating Equipment — Predictive Diagnostics — certification requirements

- **Track ID:** RE-PD-04
- **Owner:** Rotating Equipment Programme Office (RE-PO)
- **Level:** track
- **Conformance profile:** RIC-PBA-2.4
- **Last revised:** 2026-03-18

## Minimum field validation

Applicants for the Rotating Equipment — Predictive Diagnostics track must document at least
**128 supervised field-validation hours** performed on a
representative rotating-equipment test bench. Hours are logged per RIC-LOG-09
and countersigned by the applicant's quality function.

## Observed evidence from recent cycles

In the three most recent certification cycles, successful applicants documented
between 150 and 180 supervised field-validation hours. Those figures describe
observed submissions; they are not a stated requirement and must not be used as
the certification threshold.

## Relationship to other levels

The network baseline RIC-POL-03 and the Industrial Monitoring Division guidance
IM-DIV-07 remain in force for their own scopes. This page is the certification
requirement owned by the programme office responsible for track RE-PD-04
and is the requirement to cite in a submission dossier.

## Evidence checklist

1. Field-validation log (RIC-LOG-09) covering the required hours.
2. Test-bench description and calibration certificate.
3. Quality-function countersignature.
4. Fault-injection test report for the diagnostic module.
5. Configuration record of the submitted software build.
