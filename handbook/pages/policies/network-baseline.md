# RIC-POL-03 — Network-wide standards baseline

- **Owner:** RIC Network Office (RIC-NET)
- **Level:** network-wide
- **Applies to:** all member organisations and all certification programmes
- **Last revised:** 2025-11-04

## Minimum field validation

Every certification application processed anywhere in the network must document
a minimum of **40 supervised field-validation hours**.
Hours are counted under the logging rules in RIC-POL-09.

## Scope note

This baseline is the floor that applies to the whole network. Divisions and
track programme offices may publish stricter requirements for the submissions
they own; where such a requirement exists, it governs that submission.

## Evidence

Field-validation logs, countersigned by the applicant's quality function, are
required. A signed declaration alone is not accepted as evidence.
