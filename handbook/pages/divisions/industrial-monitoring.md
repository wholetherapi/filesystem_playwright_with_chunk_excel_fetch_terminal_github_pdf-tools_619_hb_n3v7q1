# IM-DIV-07 — Industrial Monitoring Division certification requirements

- **Owner:** Industrial Monitoring Division (IMD)
- **Level:** division
- **Applies to:** certification submissions processed by the Industrial Monitoring Division
- **Last revised:** 2026-01-12

## Minimum field validation

The division requires a minimum of **72 supervised field-validation
hours** for any certification submission it processes. This is stricter than
the network-wide baseline in RIC-POL-03.

## Track-specific requirements

The division hosts several certification tracks. Where a track programme office
publishes its own certification requirement, that track-level requirement
applies to submissions for that track.

## Evidence

Applicants must submit a field-validation log plus a test-bench description.
Division reviewers sample at least two logged sessions.
